## Brand & Style

The design system embodies raw kinetic intensity, relentless discipline, and precision sports engineering. Tailored for athletes, high-performance lifters, and modern gym members, the emotional tone is hyper-focused, commanding, and electro-charged.

The visual direction fuses **Modern Dark Brutalism** with **Bioluminescent Neon Tech**:
- Ultra-dense dark canvases crafted from layered obsidian and carbon mineral tones.
- High-tension typography with condensed, high-impact forms alongside muscular tabular metrics.
- High-voltage accents—Electric Blue for raw explosive power and Slate Blue for telemetry, recovery metrics, and tier gamification.
- Tactical HUD-inspired affordances: crisp, millimeter-precise borders accented by subtle ambient edge glows rather than traditional corporate drop shadows.

## Colors

The palette is engineered exclusively for an immersive, zero-fatigue dark environment optimized for high-contrast gym lighting and night training.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#1F71EF`) — Electric Blue**: Dominant energy color. Reserved for primary calls to action, high-intensity heart-rate/exertion indicators, live countdown clocks, and active lift timers.
- **Secondary (`#5F76AB`) — Slate Blue**: High-voltage success, telemetry peaks, PR breakthroughs, and completion status.
- **Tertiary (`#C55100`) — Burnt Amber**: Used for pressed states, active session locks, and aggressive alert highlights.
- **Surface & Canvas (Neutrals)**:
  - Deep Canvas: `#0A0A0C` (Absolute Obsidian baseline).
  - Elevated Container: `#121216` (Primary frame background).
  - Interactive Container / Card: `#1A1A22` (Touch surfaces).
  - Hover / Layer 2 Container: `#242430` (Interactive card states and input troughs).
- **Typography & Details**:
  - Primary Text: `#F1F1F5` (Pure Silver White for instant scanability in high-cadence contexts).
  - Secondary / Data Label: `#9E9EB2` (Muted Platinum).
  - Stroke & Keyline: `#2C2C3A` with selective contextual glowing borders.

## Typography

Typography delivers immediate legibility under strain and physical movement. 

- **Display & Headlines (`Barlow Condensed`)**: Condensed, commanding, uppercase-biased. Maximizes horizontal density, allowing full workout titles, exercise names, and intense directives to fit across small screens without wrapping awkwardly.
- **Body (`Chivo`)**: Sharp, modern grotesque with angular terminal finishes that mirror athletic precision. Delivers effortless scanning for coaching notes and routine execution instructions.
- **Telemetry & Badges (`JetBrains Mono` & `Barlow Condensed`)**: Monospaced weights provide jitter-free numeric displays during real-time rep counting, rest interval countdowns, heart rate fluctuations, and load calculations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an ultra-responsive, mobile-first fluid grid optimized for thumb reach, single-hand terminal logging, and rapid glanceability:

- **Grid Discipline**: 4-column layout on mobile (<768px), moving to 8-column for tablet (768px–1024px), and 12-column for desktop/station monitors (>1024px).
- **Rhythm & Touch Ergonomics**:
  - Touch targets maintain a minimum vertical height of 48px to accommodate chalked or shaking hands during workouts.
  - Spacing between adjacent interactive controls leverages `space-md` (1rem) to eradicate accidental mis-taps.
  - Metrics and telemetry pairs utilize tight vertical stacking with `space-xs` gap between metric titles and numeric values.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use diffuse paper drop shadows; instead, it relies on structural container layering and directional neon edge diffusion:

- **Level 0 (Canvas)**: `#0A0A0C`—the infinite gym floor.
- **Level 1 (Structural Modules)**: `#121216` surfaced with a razor-thin border (`1px solid #242430`).
- **Level 2 (Active Cards & Floating Modules)**: `#1A1A22` with a `1px solid #2C2C3A` border.
- **Level 3 (Focused & Gamified States)**:
  - *Electric Blue Glow*: Border `1px solid rgba(31, 113, 239, 0.6)` paired with ambient outer box-shadow: `0 0 16px rgba(31, 113, 239, 0.25)`.
  - *Slate Blue PR Glow*: Border `1px solid rgba(95, 118, 171, 0.7)` with outer bloom: `0 0 20px rgba(95, 118, 171, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework favors an angular, chiseled architectural contour. 

- Baseline elements (buttons, inputs, mini chips) implement a disciplined **Soft** radius of `0.25rem` (4px), projecting rugged mechanical precision.
- Structural container cards utilize `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Data pills, status indicators, and gamification tier badges utilize hard-capsule or angled chamfered shapes to evoke military and industrial fitness equipment.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Beast Mode" Action)**: Background `#1F71EF`, text `#0A0A0C`, uppercase `Barlow Condensed` bold font. Hover/active shifts to `#C55100` accompanied by an intense Electric Blue glow (`0 0 16px rgba(31, 113, 239, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary (PR / Milestone Action)**: Background `transparent`, border `1.5px solid #5F76AB`, text `#5F76AB`. Hover/active shifts to solid `#5F76AB` with text `#0A0A0C`.
- **Tertiary / Subdued**: Background `#242430`, text `#F1F1F5`, border `1px solid #2C2C3A`.

### Loyalty & Tier Gamification Badges
Dynamic high-contrast status badges tracking lifter progression:
- **NOVATO (Rookie)**: Charcoal frame `#1A1A22`, text `#9E9EB2`, border `#2C2C3A`.
- **GUERRERO (Warrior)**: Deep silver-tinted container with subtle metallic border and brushed edge highlights.
- **BESTIA (Beast)**: Primary Electric Blue border `#1F71EF` with persistent faint blue pulse glow.
- **ANIMAL**: Pure obsidian base with multi-chromatic perimeter shimmer combining `#1F71EF` and `#5F76AB`, intense electric luminescence, bold monospaced ranking index.

### Metric Cards & Telemetry Logs
- Background `#1A1A22`, padded with `space-md`. Top line displays title in `JetBrains Mono` (`#9E9EB2`, uppercase). 
- Primary stat displayed in `metric-huge` (`#F1F1F5`).
- Secondary delta indicators (+5kg, -2s) rendered in `#5F76AB` alongside micro trend arrows.

### Input Fields & Set Trackers
- Darkened inset surface `#121216`, border `1px solid #2C2C3A`. Text `#F1F1F5`.
- Active focus state: border shifts immediately to `#1F71EF` with high-contrast caret and zero latency.
- Numeric quick-tap steppers (+ / -) feature 48px tactile tap surfaces for effortless single-finger incrementation.

### Chips & Filters
- Compact horizontal scroll group. Unselected chips: `#1A1A22` text `#9E9EB2`. Selected: `#1F71EF` background with `#0A0A0C` text, instantly marking muscles targeted or active workout categories.
